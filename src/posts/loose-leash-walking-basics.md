Pulling works for dogs because it gets them where they want to go. Loose-leash walking changes that.

## Stop when the leash goes tight

Stand still until there's slack, then carry on.

## Reward the right spot

Give a treat when your dog is next to your leg.

## Short sessions

Practice five minutes at a time, in a quiet place first.
