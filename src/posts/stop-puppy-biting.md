Puppies explore the world with their mouths. Biting is normal, but they need to learn how gentle to be.

## Redirect to a toy

When teeth touch skin, offer a chew toy instead and praise when they take it.

## Calm, not rough

Rough play winds puppies up. Short, calm sessions work better.

## Naps matter

Overtired puppies bite more. Most need 18 hours of sleep a day.
